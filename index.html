FAQ

Questions & answers.



Does it work on both Upwork and Fiverr? ▼
Yes. FreelanceTrust automatically activates on any Upwork freelancer profile and any Fiverr seller page.
Do I need to create an account? ▼
No account needed. Install the extension and it works instantly — no signup, no email, no login.
How is the Trust Score calculated? ▼
The score is based on Job Success Score, client ratings, number of completed jobs, account age, hours worked, and keyword analysis of reviews. Everything is transparent — you see exactly what contributed to the score.
What's the daily limit on the free plan? ▼
Free users can check up to 20 profiles per day. The counter resets at midnight. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited checks.
Does the extension read or store my data? ▼
FreelanceTrust only reads publicly visible profile data on the page. No personal data is collected or sent anywhere. The daily counter is stored locally in your browser.
